Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if you put too much dough in a Pullman pan? +
If you overfill a lidded Pullman pan, the expanding dough will have no room to rise. The intense internal pressure forces wet dough through the pan's corner vents, producing dense, rubbery, compacted crumb with gummy corners, and potentially jamming the sliding lid shut.
How high should dough rise in an open-top sandwich pan before baking? +
For an open-top sandwich pan, proof until the center of the dough dome crests approximately 1 inch (2.5 cm) above the rim of the pan. If you bake too early, the bread will tear along the sides; if you wait until it rises 2 inches above the rim, the dough will collapse during the initial heat blast in the oven.
