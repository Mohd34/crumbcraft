Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection & Privacy Commitment

At CrumbCraft (accessible via https://mohd34.github.io/crumbcraft), privacy is our core engineering principle. All recipe math, baking calculations, and temperature inputs are performed 100% locally on your device in client-side JavaScript.

2. Cookies & Tracking

CrumbCraft does not use invasive third-party tracking cookies, fingerprinting mechanisms, or advertising tracking scripts. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

3. Local Storage

If you choose to save custom dough formulas or preferred units (Celsius vs Fahrenheit), this data is stored strictly within your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to any external server.
