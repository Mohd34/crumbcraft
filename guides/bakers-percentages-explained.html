Baker's Percentages Explained: The Master Formula

Why do professional bakers and artisan bread makers calculate recipes so that percentages add up to 180% or 200% instead of 100%? Here is the complete mathematical and practical explanation.

What is Baker's Percentage?

In conventional home cooking, ingredient percentages are calculated based on the total mass of the final mixture, meaning all ingredients sum to 100%. If a cake weighs 1000g and contains 200g of sugar, sugar is 20% of the recipe.

In professional bread baking, however, total flour weight is always anchored as the 100% baseline. Every other ingredient—water, salt, starter, yeast, oil, or seeds—is expressed as a ratio of that flour:

Ingredient Baker's % = (Ingredient Weight ÷ Total Flour Weight) × 100%

A Concrete Example: The Standard Country Loaf

Consider a classic artisan sourdough formula:

  • Bread Flour: 500g (100.0%)
  • Water: 375g (75.0% hydration)
  • Active Levain: 100g (20.0%)
  • Fine Sea Salt: 10g (2.0%)

Sum of Baker's Percentages: 100% + 75% + 20% + 2% = 197.0%.
Total Dough Weight: 500g + 375g + 100g + 10g = 985g.

Why Baker's Math is a Superpower

  1. Effortless Scaling: Whether baking 1 loaf (500g flour) or 200 loaves (100,000g flour), you simply multiply the flour weight by each ingredient's percentage.
  2. Instant Dough Assessment: Just by reading "78% hydration, 20% starter, 2% salt", an experienced baker immediately knows the dough will be wet, extensible, and take roughly 5–6 hours to bulk ferment at room temperature.
  3. Troubleshooting & Adjustments: If you want a crisper crust or more open crumb, you adjust only one percentage variable (e.g. increase hydration from 72% to 76%) without having to recalculate every other ingredient in the recipe.

Reverse Scaling: Target Loaf Weight to Ingredients

Suppose your banneton basket holds an 850g loaf. How much flour, water, starter, and salt do you need?

Let $W_{\text{target}}$ be 850g. Using the formula:

Flour Weight = Target Weight ÷ (1 + Hydration% + Starter% + Salt%)

With 75% hydration (0.75), 20% starter (0.20), and 2% salt (0.02):
Flour = 850 ÷ (1 + 0.75 + 0.20 + 0.02) = 850 ÷ 1.97 = 431.5g

  • Water: 431.5 × 0.75 = 323.6g
  • Starter: 431.5 × 0.20 = 86.3g
  • Salt: 431.5 × 0.02 = 8.6g
