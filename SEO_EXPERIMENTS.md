## 2. Hypothesis-Driven SEO Experiments

Rule: Only change ONE meaningful variable per experiment. Never change titles, content, schema, and internal links simultaneously.

- **Hypothesis:** Prominently highlighting `"Ice Water Math"` and `"3 & 4 Factor Sourdough"` in the `<title>` tag and meta description of `ddt-calculator.html` will achieve a click-through rate (CTR) at least 50% higher than generic "water temperature calculator" titles once impressions begin accumulating in Google Search Console.
- **Current State:** Baseline title set to: `Desired Dough Temperature (DDT) Calculator & Ice Water Math | CrumbCraft`.
- **Change:** Specific focus on solving the summer baking ice water problem.
- **Date Initiated:** 2026-09-24
- **Affected URL:** `https://mohd34.github.io/crumbcraft/ddt-calculator.html`
- **Expected Result:** Position < 10 for `"ddt calculator sourdough"` within 30 days of first indexing; CTR > 6.0%.
- **Actual Result:** Pending initial crawl & impression accumulation.
- **Decision:** Active in Production.

### Experiment 002: Contextual Internal Link Bridge Between Hydration and Starter Planner

- **Current State:** Navigation links exist in header tabs; contextual in-content link added directly below the 3-Way Hydration callout box.
- **Change:** Single contextual hyperlink: *"Planning your levain build? Use the Starter Feeding Schedule to time peak activity."*
- **Date Initiated:** 2026-09-24
- **Affected URL:** `https://mohd34.github.io/crumbcraft/` -> `https://mohd34.github.io/crumbcraft/starter-feeding-schedule.html`
- **Expected Result:** Average session duration > 2.0 minutes; faster indexation of `starter-feeding-schedule.html`.
- **Actual Result:** Pending crawl data.
- **Decision:** Active in Production.
