Commercial Yeast & Leavening Conversion Chart

Commercial baker's yeast comes in three primary forms, each containing different moisture levels and living cell counts per gram:

Yeast Type Moisture % Live Cell Density Blooming Required? Conversion Ratio
Instant Dry Yeast (IDY) ~5% Very High (fast porous drying) No. Mix directly into dry flour. 1.0× (Baseline)
Active Dry Yeast (ADY) ~8% Moderate (outer shell of dead cells) Recommended. Dissolve in lukewarm water. 1.25× (IDY × 1.25)
Fresh / Cake Yeast ~70% High (hydrated living cells) Crumble into warm water or milk. 3.0× (IDY × 3.0)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I substitute Instant Dry Yeast directly for Active Dry Yeast? +
Yes! If a recipe calls for 10 grams of Active Dry Yeast, use 8 grams of Instant Dry Yeast (10g × 0.8 = 8g). You do not need to proof or bloom Instant Dry Yeast in water with sugar beforehand; you can whisk it directly into your dry flour.
Will sourdough discard make my cookies or pancakes taste sour? +
Discard that has sat in the refrigerator for only 1 to 4 days has very mild, buttery lactic acidity that enhances grain flavor without being sharply sour. If your discard has sat for weeks and has a gray layer of hooch (alcohol) on top, it will have a very sharp acetic punch.
